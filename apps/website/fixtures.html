Field prep timeline

Every Pirates home fixture across all six teams, laid out season-long. The U10 Diamond is only ever used by U10, so the thing that actually matters for prep is whether the C-Field is needed alongside the Main Diamond.

Home fixture Away fixture C-Field also needed that day
How fields are assigned: U10 always play the U10 Diamond (8am) — no other team can use it, so it's never a prep concern. U15 always open the Main Diamond (8am). U18 follow at 10am on the Main Diamond when home. U12 take the Main Diamond at 10am when U18 are away — otherwise they move to the C-Field. Second League always have the Main Diamond when they're home, kicking off at 12:45; if Masters are also home that day, Masters move to the C-Field at 13:15. If Masters are home alone, they take the Main Diamond at 13:15. All games run 2 hours.
