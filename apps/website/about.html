Welcome to the Helderberg Pirates

Since 1952

Welcome to the Helderberg Pirates Baseball Club, where the love of baseball and vibrant community spirit meet.

  • About Us
  • Why Choose Us

The Helderberg Pirates Baseball Club is an amateur baseball club situated at Radloff Park Somerset West. The Helderberg Pirates Baseball ‘family’ is dedicated to the training of adults and children in the sport of baseball within the Helderberg District. Helderberg Pirates was originally started in 1952 and was original known as Strand / Somerset West Baseball Club. The club originally operated from the ‘Old Rugby Club’ after which it moved to the Charles Morkel stadium in Strand. Then after a short stint at the old De Beers AFC soccer fields, the club eventually found its own home at Radloff Park in 1995. Since moving to the new grounds a clubhouse with change-rooms, hot showers, events hall, batting cages and a licenced bar has been established while dugouts (where player’s sit during games) and a scorers box have also been constructed on the main field.

At our club, baseball is more than just a game – it’s a community. We provide a welcoming environment where players of all ages and skill levels can learn, grow, and thrive both on and off the field. Our dedicated coaches focus on building strong fundamentals while encouraging teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship. Whether you’re looking to develop your skills, make new friends, or simply enjoy the spirit of the game, our club offers the perfect balance of fun, development, and competitive opportunity. Join us and become part of a team that feels like family.

Respect

On the diamond, respect means honoring the game, your teammates, opponents, and umpires — playing hard but fair, and recognizing the effort everyone puts in.

Openness

Baseball thrives when players are open to learning, trying new roles, and sharing ideas, because every swing, pitch, and play is a chance to grow.


Community

A team is more than nine players — it’s families, friends, and fans coming together, building a supportive community that cheers every run and pick-off.

Kindness

Kindness in baseball is helping a rookie with their glove, offering encouragement after a strikeout, and celebrating others’ successes as much as your own.

Support

Every player needs a strong bench behind them — support means backing each other up on the field and in life, knowing your team has your back no matter the score.
